One request, start to finish

Say what you need. It sees it through.

Kelo searches, checks with you, finishes the job and looks back on the day. You can follow every step on your phone.

  1. 01

    Ask in words or by voice

    Type or talk. Send photos and PDF, Word or Excel files along with it. Kelo answers in the language you wrote in.

  2. 02

    It searches in its own browser

    Kelo opens sites, compares and fills in forms in its own Chromium, inside the Kelo server. Watch it live from your phone, take over, and hand it back. That browser is walled off from your home network.

  3. 03

    It asks before anything final

    Paying, ordering, booking, sending, deleting: anything hard to undo sends an approval card to your phone first, saying in plain words what's about to happen. Nothing moves until you allow it.

  4. 04

    It looks back every night

    At the end of the day it reads back over your conversations and suggests short rules about how you like things done. Keep or reject each one; a rejected rule is never suggested again.

What it does

From one chat line to files and reminders

Kelo starts with a conversation, makes the files, keeps track of the schedule and speaks up when something needs you.

Schedules · watchers

On its own, when you said

Say “around 9 every morning” or “every 30 minutes” and it runs then. It wakes only when a page shows certain words or a price drops below your number, and those checks don't use an AI model.

Keychain

Passwords never go through the chat

Passwords and API keys go in through a form in the app and stay locked on your Kelo server. Nobody can read what you store, and the assistant uses the values without seeing them.

Payment cards

It pays only when you allow it

Choose per card whether it may pay on its own. Cards without auto-pay are locked with a key only your phone holds, so even your server can't read them, and each payment needs your fingerprint or face.

Goals

Goals become plans

Write down a goal and Kelo splits it into what you do and what it does, and keeps track of the numbers and the deadline.

Many phones, many people

One server per person, any number of phones

A Kelo server is one person's assistant. To share Kelo, give each person a server of their own.

  • One server per person

    Chats, memory, keys and cards are kept fully apart. Nobody sees anyone else's conversations.

  • Any number of phones

    Pair as many phones and desktop apps with one server as you like. One QR scan each.

  • Several copies on one machine

    The machine that runs your server can run several copies of Kelo. Each copy has its own address and its own data.

  • Resources in numbers

    Set the RAM, CPU and disk each copy may use, or let it use what the machine has.

Privacy

Your data stays on your server

Your chats, notes, saved keys and cards are stored only on the Kelo server you run. The developer has no way into it and does not collect your conversations.

Your messages are sent to the AI provider you choose (for example Anthropic or Google) to get a reply. The source is public, so anyone can check all of this.

Host Kelo on a free cloud server

Tip · Run it for free

Oracle Cloud Always Free for Kelo

Oracle Cloud's Always Free tier includes an Arm server with 2 OCPUs and 12 GB of memory that stays free, and that is enough for Kelo. Install Kelo there and reach it with a Kelo address (offered for now). Then run FreeLLMAPI next to it: it brings the free tiers of many AI providers together behind one OpenAI-compatible address, which you enter in the app under My → Model → Custom endpoint.

  • Oracle may reclaim a free server that stays mostly idle for 7 days (CPU, network and memory all under 20%). Upgrading the account to Pay As You Go keeps Always Free resources free of charge, and is commonly reported to prevent this.
  • Free models are slower and less reliable at using tools than paid ones.
  • Oracle's, FreeLLMAPI's and each AI provider's own terms apply.

FAQ

Questions people ask

More in the install guide.

What kind of machine do I need?

A Mac, Linux or Windows machine that runs Docker. The minimum is 2 GB RAM · 2 CPU cores · 10 GB free disk; 4 GB RAM · 4 CPU cores · 20 GB free disk is recommended.

How do I pay for the AI model?

You pay the AI company you chose directly, with your own API key. Kelo doesn't sit in the middle. A local model costs nothing extra.

What happens when the server's machine is off?

The assistant rests too. When it's back, a scheduled run missed within the last two hours runs once; older ones wait for their next time.

I got a link to connect my phone, and this page opened.

Open the Kelo app, then scan the QR code on the connect screen or paste the link into ‘Paste link’ as it is. The link works once, for 10 minutes.

Do my chats go to the people who make Kelo?

No. Your conversation travels only between your Kelo server and the AI company that writes the replies. Only a reply you send yourself with “Report this reply” reaches the developer.

Can my family use it too?

Yes. Run one copy of Kelo per person on the same machine, and chats and keys stay fully apart.

Does it work on iPhone?

The iPhone app is being built. Android, Windows and macOS come first.

Which languages does it speak?

The app and the server's messages come in 20 languages, English and Korean among them.
