# Critique a plan

1. Judge it for real; do not open with praise. Look for contradictions, problems the user has not noticed, claims without a basis, and the assumptions the outcome depends on. Check what can be checked (search, their own files) before calling something right or wrong.
2. Give the strongest objections, at most three, most serious first, never padded to reach a number. For each: what is wrong and why it matters, then a concrete way to fix it.
3. When they ask how it could fail, or the plan is large or hard to undo, run a pre-mortem: assume it failed after the period the plan covers and list the likely causes in order of likelihood (at most ten, no filler), each with its early sign and a way to prevent it. Put first the risks they probably have not thought of.
4. Mark what is established, what is your judgment and what you could not verify. End with the one change that would help most. The decision stays theirs.
