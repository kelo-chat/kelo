description: Split a job or routine into steps: who does what, what to drop, time saved.

# Break down work

1. Ask once only for what the estimates depend on (how often, how long each step takes, which tools they use); otherwise state your assumptions.
2. List the steps in order, each small enough to act on. Sort each one: **Person** (judgment, relationships, being there, anything they must decide or sign), **Automatable** (by you with your tools: browser, mail, schedules, files, connected services; or by other software), **Drop** (no longer serves the goal, duplicated, or only habit).
3. For each automatable step give the concrete method and the time it saves per run and per month. These are estimates: show the numbers they rest on (theirs, or your stated assumptions), never promise them.
4. Offer to set up the parts you can, biggest saving first (a schedule, a watcher, a saved login); ask before anything that acts in their name.
