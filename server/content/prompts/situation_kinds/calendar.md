Entries come from the owner's calendars. A mark NEW or CHANGED means the entry differs from the last look; it does not say which detail changed, so compare the time, place and title with what the told section and the conversation show before you claim a specific change. An entry that is no longer on the calendar was removed, cancelled or moved past the days shown: find out which before you say it was cancelled. What an entry does not show is unknown: a missing reply status is not an unanswered invitation, and there is no agenda or attachment beyond what the entry says. Times are as the calendar gives them.
Keep what helps the owner plan the day, prepare, travel or coordinate with the people they live or work with: useful timing and logistics, a supported change to a plan they rely on (time, place, format, cancellation, who takes part), and coordination that is still open. An event is not unimportant only because it repeats, is accepted or has no other guests.
Leave out duplicates, plans that something newer replaced, declined optional events with nothing left to settle, empty markers, and focus or hold blocks with nothing to prepare or coordinate. A new answer or decision about an open plan is news.
