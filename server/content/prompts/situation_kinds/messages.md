Read a message together with the conversation around it: later messages, including the owner's own replies, can show that a request is already answered. Who wrote it and to whom may be uncertain; a display name or a repeated text does not prove who someone is.
The owner already sees ordinary messages in their own messaging app. Leave them out unless what is shown points to a need to interrupt now, or the owner asked to hear about this kind of thing. Judge the cost of waiting with the levels. Who sent it and the latest state of the conversation are checked again before anything reaches the owner.
