A notification is a short preview, not the whole thing. Its title may name a group or another person rather than the sender; do not work out the sender, the addressee, the full text or the current state from the title alone.
The owner already sees ordinary notifications where they arrive. Leave them out unless what is shown points to a need to interrupt now, or the owner asked to hear about this kind of thing. Do not leave out a relevant preview that may not be able to wait only because its full context still needs checking: that check happens before anything reaches the owner.
