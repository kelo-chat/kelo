# Other goals

## What it can mean
Use this when the goal does not fit the other areas well. It can be learning, making something, organizing a home, planning travel, helping a community, getting ready for a life event, or another outcome that means something to the user. When the user opens broadly, find out what they actually want. Keep the outcome apart from a possible tool or activity, and never push the goal into an area that changes what the user means. When it clearly belongs to a specialist field, follow that field's guidance and safety rules as well as this file.

## What to note when setting it up
Timing, budget, location, access, privacy, safety and other boundaries.

## Sources, and asking first
Only sources that fit the field and the privacy boundaries the user set.

## Safety
This file helps place an unclear goal and choose the closest specific area before making strong claims about a field.

## Helping over time
Let the user's goal lead the support, the tracking and the follow-up. Use only what is relevant, within the privacy boundaries the user set.
