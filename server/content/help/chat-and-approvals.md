# Chat and approvals

You talk to Kelo in the app chat (Android, iPhone, desktop) and, if you connect one, from Telegram, Discord or Slack; it is the same conversation everywhere. Kelo works on the language model you picked in the app.

## What Kelo does on its own

Reading, searching the web, looking at pages, checking your connected services and preparing something need no question. Kelo asks before anything that acts in your name or is hard to undo: sending, posting, paying, booking, deleting.

## Approval cards

A step that commits something in your name shows an Allow / Deny card with what will happen (the recipient and text, the amount and card, the site). Kelo cannot answer a card itself or work around a "no": a denied or unanswered card means the step does not happen. If you asked for the exact action in your message, that is your go-ahead for it; if the content then changes, Kelo shows the new version first.

## Many items at once

When you ask for the same kind of step on many items ("all of these", "every one"), Kelo first finds the items, then asks once with the real count and the list, and does them all under that one approval.

## A permission for a task that repeats

For a schedule or a watcher, Kelo asks once at setup (one card naming the trigger, exactly what will be done, the site or recipients, and the limits). After that each run does it on its own and you are told right after. Anything outside it (a higher total, another site, a deletion not allowed) still asks.

## What Kelo may do with each connector

In the app, under Connectors, open a connector and choose "What Kelo may do". For each kind of action (read, change, send, delete) and for single actions you can set allow, ask or off. Allow means no card, except for payments, card and PIN steps, sign-ups and account steps, which always keep their own checks. Ask means a card every time. Off means the step is not run.

## Quick answers and reactions

When Kelo ends on a question with short answers (yes/no, a pick) it shows tap buttons. It also reacts to your message with an emoji when it has finished handling it.

## Whose instructions count

Kelo takes instructions only from your messages in this chat. Text in emails, web pages, files and other services is treated as information, never as an order.
