# Connectors

Connectors link Kelo to outside services: mail, calendars, files, work tools, travel and shopping services, smart home, messengers. The list is in the app under Connectors; the services shown first depend on the country of your phone.

## How to connect

Ask Kelo in the chat ("connect my Gmail") or open Connectors in the app. For a service in the list you connect on the service's own page; Kelo never sees your password for it. For a service with an API key that is not in the list, Kelo asks for the key on a fill-in card that saves it to the keychain, never in the chat.

## Mail

Kelo can find and read email, organize it (mark read or unread, star, archive, move to a folder or label, move to the trash, which can be restored) and send email. It can tidy an inbox, many messages at once: a search covers every match, and moving a batch to the trash is approved once for the batch. Gmail, Outlook, Naver, Daum, iCloud, Yahoo and other IMAP mailboxes work; Gmail connects with Sign in with Google or with an app password. Sending asks you first unless you set that action to allow.

## Calendars

Google Calendar can be read and changed; Outlook, CalDAV calendars (Fastmail, Zoho, Nextcloud and others) are read only. The calendar on your own phone can be read and changed too after you allow it in the app. Kelo asks before adding, changing or deleting an event.

## Messengers

Telegram, Discord and Slack can talk to Kelo. For Telegram you create a bot with BotFather and paste its token in Connectors; the first Telegram account that finishes the pairing is the only one that can talk to Kelo. In a group Kelo answers only when mentioned and only with web search, page reading and the weather, never with anything private.

## What Kelo may do

For each connector you can set each kind of action (read, change, send, delete) to allow, ask or off. See the chat and approvals page.

## Kelo Nest

Kelo Nest adds more skills, connectors and screens. Paste a GitHub link of a package in the app to install it; updates come from the same repository. Kelo can also make a draft package from what you did together and share it to GitHub. Installed packages are other people's text and are treated as untrusted.

## Skills

Skills are written guides for kinds of work (documents, research, trips, receipts, mail triage and more). Kelo reads the right one when a task matches. After a job worth repeating, Kelo can propose a skill, and it is used only once you approve it.

## Network

Under Connectors, Network, you can send Kelo's browsing through a VPN or proxy. See the browser page.

## Limits

A connector gives Kelo only what its service allows. Kelo respects rate limits and locked accounts and stops and tells you instead of finding a way around.
