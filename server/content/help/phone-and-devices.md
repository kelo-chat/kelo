# Phone and devices

Kelo runs on your Kelo server; the app on your phone or computer is how you talk to it. Apps exist for Android, iPhone and desktop with the same features, apart from what a platform does not allow.

## Pairing the phone

The app needs an https address for your server and a pairing (a QR code from the server setup, or a link). One server can pair several phones and computers. See the server and updates page for the ways to reach the server.

## What Kelo can use on your phone

Only what you share. After you allow it in the app, Kelo can read your phone's calendar, contacts, text messages, call history, notifications and the health summaries you chose to share, and it can add, change or delete calendar events. Sending a text message needs your approval of the recipient and the whole text. Calling opens the dialer with the number filled in and you press call; Kelo never places a call itself.

## Location

The app sends a coarse location when it is opened, once you allow it. Kelo uses it for the weather, for places near you and for "where am I". Place names and nearby places come from OpenStreetMap data; coordinates are rounded before they leave the server. When no place is known Kelo asks on a card.

## Notifications

Results of schedules, watches and background jobs, approval cards that wait for you, and reminders arrive as notifications. A push carries only an id, never the text; the app asks your own server for the details. You can set what Kelo tells you and when.

## Pictures and files

You can send photos and documents in the chat. Kelo reads documents (PDF, Word, Excel and more) and looks at photos if your model can see images. Files Kelo makes land in your files in the app. Kelo can also build documents and convert files in a private work room that has no internet access.

## An Android device for Kelo

If you run an Android device or emulator for Kelo (a container on a Linux server, an emulator, or a spare phone with wireless debugging), Kelo can read its screen and tap, type, swipe and open apps on it. It is for apps with no usable website. Your own phone is never driven this way.

## Several copies

One computer can host more than one Kelo server, each with its own data and phone pairings.
