# Payments

Kelo can buy, order and book for you on websites in its own browser, and it always asks you before money moves. Payments are never done silently.

## The flow

1. You ask. Kelo asks every missing choice once, with prices. If you did not name an exact item, it offers two to four fitting ones as a choice and does not pick for you.
2. Kelo opens the site in its browser, signs in with a login you saved, and prepares the order: the cart, the options, the delivery address.
3. Before paying, Kelo checks the order against what you asked (items, variants, quantities, delivery, dates, cancellation terms) and shows an approval card with what you are buying, the total and the card.
4. Only after you tap Allow does Kelo press the pay button. It then reports the confirmation number from the page.

## What an approval covers

One approval covers that checkout on that shop's own address while the currency stays the same and the total and any extra charge stay within the amounts you saw. A higher total, another currency or another shop needs a new approval. A change of items, delivery, dates or terms needs a new order check even if the price is lower.

## If the result is unknown

If the page does not show whether the order went through, Kelo does not press pay again and does not buy a replacement. It looks for the order again about ten minutes later and once more half an hour after that, and if it still cannot tell it asks you to look in your mail or the shop account.

## Cards and addresses

Kelo can pay with a card you saved for it (it lists the cards you have) or ask you for one. Card numbers are never typed in chat. Delivery uses the shop account's address, a shipping profile you saved, or Kelo asks where to deliver. Some payments need extra verification (a code in a banking app, a PIN); you finish that step on your phone.

## Safer approvals

In your profile you can require that payment approvals carry a signature from your phone's secure hardware, made behind a fingerprint or face check. Then even a stolen device token cannot approve a payment.

## Buying later, on a repeat or the moment something appears

Kelo can set up a schedule or a watcher that buys within limits you set: the most per purchase, how many times, which card. You approve that once on a permission card; after that a run can buy on its own within those limits and tells you right after. A higher price, another shop or another card still asks.

## What Kelo will not do

Kelo does not refuse a purchase because it assumes a site or the law requires something; it opens the site and tries, and says it cannot be done only after the site itself refuses.
