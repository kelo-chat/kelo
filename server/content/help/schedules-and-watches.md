# Schedules and watches

Kelo can do things later, on a repeat, or when something happens. There are three ways, and Kelo picks the one that reacts in time.

## Schedules

A schedule runs a job at a set time or on a repeat (every day, every weekday, every N minutes) in your time zone: a reminder, a report, a check, a routine. You approve a schedule when it is made. Kelo writes the job's instruction so it works alone later. Ask Kelo to list, change, pause or delete your schedules, or to show what past runs did.

## What a run does and what it tells you

A run works unattended: it cannot message you while it works. When it ends, Kelo makes one decision: nothing to tell, or tell you with a short text. A run that failed, or left a step unconfirmed, is always told. If you asked to hear only about changes or problems, a run with nothing new stays quiet. A run blocked for a while (a site down for maintenance) tries again later on its own; a lasting block (a login is gone) pauses the schedule and tells you.

## Watches

A watcher checks one thing every so often without using the AI model and wakes Kelo only when its condition holds: a seat that opens, a price that drops, a page that changes, a booking that appears. It starts switched off. Kelo first does a dry run to show you what it finds, and you approve turning it on. A watcher only reads; it never orders or pays by itself. It can read pages you are signed in to. The most often a watch checks is every 5 minutes, with a little random variation so it does not look like a bot.

## When X happens, do Y

For "if it rains, remind me" or "buy it when it is back in stock" Kelo sets up the schedule or watcher, then asks once for a permission card that names the trigger, the action and the limits (the most to spend, how many times, which card, which recipients). Once allowed it happens on its own and you are told right after; anything outside the permission asks.

## Morning briefing

The morning briefing is built in (weather, today's calendar and what was missed). Ask Kelo to change its time or what it contains; it is not a schedule you need to make.

## Things coming up

For things coming up in your life (a flight, a parcel, a booking), Kelo can look after them: it watches the parts that can change and tells you when something changes.

## Honesty about "later"

Kelo says it will tell or do something later only when it has created the schedule, watcher or job that will do it. Checks run on a schedule, give or take a few minutes, so Kelo does not promise to catch something the moment it happens.

## Notifications

Results arrive as a message in the chat and as a notification on your phone. A push notification carries only an id; the app then asks your own server for the details.
