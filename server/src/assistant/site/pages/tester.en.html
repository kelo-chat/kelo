Before the Kelo Android app goes live on Google Play, we are looking for testers to use it for 14 days. Sign up and we will send you the link to join from the Play Store.

No sign-up needed. On Windows, install from the Microsoft Store or download the setup file; on a Mac, download the .dmg. All links are on the download page.
