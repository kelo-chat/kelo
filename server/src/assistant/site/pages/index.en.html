Kelo pays only with your OK

Kelo keeps passwords out of the chat

Passwords and API keys go into a form, never the chat. The assistant uses them without ever seeing them.

Works with your other self-hosted apps

Connect Kelo to the apps you already run at home by their address on your network or Tailscale, for example http://192.168.0.10:8123. Kelo asks them directly: nothing goes through us, and nothing leaves your network.

Home Assistant, Frigate, Immich, Paperless-ngx, Mealie, Tandoor, Grocy, Actual Budget, Firefly III, Linkwarden, Karakeep, Wallabag, Miniflux, FreshRSS, n8n, Uptime Kuma, Beszel, AdGuard Home, Pi-hole, Nextcloud, Gitea, Forgejo.

Ask for something in your own message, like “unlock the front door” or “turn the heating off”, and Kelo does it right away. It asks first when it would act on its own, or when the request came from text in a page, mail or message.

Kelo never connects to itself or to a cloud server’s own settings: addresses that point back at the Kelo server (127.0.0.1) or at a cloud metadata service (169.254.x.x) are refused. Coming later: https addresses with a self-signed certificate, such as Frigate’s signed-in port 8971.

Host Kelo on a free cloud server

Tip · Run it for free

Oracle Cloud Always Free for Kelo

Oracle Cloud's Always Free tier includes an Arm server with 2 OCPUs and 12 GB of memory that stays free, and that is enough for Kelo. Install Kelo there and reach it with a Kelo address (offered for now). Then run FreeLLMAPI next to it: it brings the free tiers of many AI providers together behind one OpenAI-compatible address, which you enter in the app under My → Model → Custom endpoint.

  • Oracle may reclaim a free server that stays mostly idle for 7 days (CPU, network and memory all under 20%). Upgrading the account to Pay As You Go keeps Always Free resources free of charge, and is commonly reported to prevent this.
  • Free models are slower and less reliable at using tools than paid ones.
  • Oracle's, FreeLLMAPI's and each AI provider's own terms apply.

Questions people ask

More in the install guide.

{{svg:thinking}}

  • What computer does Kelo need?

    A Mac, Linux or Windows computer that runs Docker, from 2 GB RAM · 2 CPU cores · 10 GB free disk.

  • How do I pay for Kelo’s AI model?

    You pay the AI company you chose directly, with your own API key. Kelo doesn’t sit in the middle.

  • What happens when the Kelo server is off?

    The assistant rests too. A scheduled run missed in the last two hours runs once when it’s back.

  • A Kelo pairing link opened this page. Now what?

    Open the Kelo app and scan the QR code, or paste the link into ‘Paste link’. It works once, for 10 minutes.

  • Do my chats go to Kelo?

    No. They travel only between your Kelo server and the AI company that writes the replies.

  • Does Kelo work on iPhone?

    Yes. The iPhone app is on the App Store (iOS 17 or later).

  • Which languages does Kelo speak?

    The app and the server’s messages come in 20 languages, English and Korean among them.
