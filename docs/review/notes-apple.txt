ABOUT KELO
Kelo is the app for a personal AI assistant that its owner runs on their own server (self-hosted). The app itself holds no
assistant: it connects to the owner's server, and everything happens there. For this review we run a demo server just for
{store} reviewers, with an AI model already set up, so you can use the app right away.

1. Open Kelo. The first screen is "Get started".
2. Tap "Paste link".

WHAT TO TRY
- Chat: ask anything, for example "What can you do?", "Plan a 3-day trip to Seoul" or "Summarize the news about electric cars".
  Replies come from a real AI model, so they take a few seconds.
- Connectors (the Connectors tab): the services Kelo can work with (calendars, mail, GitHub, Notion and more). Connecting one is
  optional and uses your own account on that service; nothing is needed for the review.
- Schedules: ask "Every morning at 8, tell me the weather in Seoul" or "Remind me in 5 minutes to stretch". The result arrives
  in the chat and as a notification at that time.
- My tab: the model in use, devices, and the app's settings.

PURCHASES
Kelo can prepare an order on a website, but it always shows an Allow / Deny card with the item and the total before anything that
costs money, and it never pays without that approval. No payment card is saved on this demo server.

REPORTING A REPLY
Touch and hold any reply from Kelo and choose "Report", pick the reason and send it. Reports go to the Kelo developer.
