# Kelo Contributor License Agreement

> **DRAFT — for the owner and a lawyer to review. Not in force. Do not sign.**
>
> **Model.** This agreement follows the structure of the Apache Software Foundation's Individual and Corporate
> Contributor License Agreements, version 2.0 (copyright licence, patent licence with defensive termination,
> representations, disclaimer), rewritten in plain language for Kelo. Section 3 adds an outbound-licensing clause of the
> kind offered by the Harmony Agreements' "any license" option (HA-CLA-I-ANY / HA-CLA-E-ANY), so that the Licensor may
> offer contributions under the AGPL **and** under commercial terms (dual licensing). Section 4 adds a commitment that
> the community edition stays available under the AGPL.
>
> Placeholders in `[BRACKETS]` must be completed before use.

This document has two parts:

- **Part A: Individual agreement.** For a person who contributes on their own behalf.
- **Part B: Entity agreement.** For a company or other organisation whose employees or contractors contribute as part of
  their work. An entity signs Part B once and lists the people who may contribute for it (Schedule A); each listed person
  still signs Part A through the pull-request bot so their GitHub account can be matched.

## Part B — Entity Contributor License Agreement

This Part B applies when a company or other legal entity (the **"Entity"**) allows its employees or contractors to make
Contributions as part of their work.

### B1. Definitions

The definitions in Part A, Section 1 apply, with these additions:

- **"You"** means the Entity accepting this Part B, together with every entity that controls, is controlled by, or is
  under common control with it. "Control" means direct or indirect ownership of more than fifty per cent of the voting
  securities or other ownership interest, or the power to direct its management.
- **"Designated Contributors"** means the individuals listed in Schedule A, as updated under Section B4.

### B2. Grants

The Entity grants the Licensor and recipients of software distributed by the Licensor every licence and permission set
out in Part A, Sections 2, 3, 5 and 6, for all Contributions Submitted by Designated Contributors on the Entity's behalf.
The Licensor's commitment in Part A, Section 4 applies to those Contributions as well.

### B3. Representations

The Entity represents that:

1. It is legally entitled to grant these licences, and the person signing below is authorised to bind the Entity.
2. Each Designated Contributor is authorised to Submit Contributions on the Entity's behalf.
3. The representations in Part A, Section 7, items 2, 4 and 5, are true for each Contribution its Designated
   Contributors Submit.

### B4. Changes to Schedule A

The Entity's authorised contact may add or remove Designated Contributors by e-mail to support@kelo.chat. Removal stops
future Contributions from being covered; licences for Contributions already Submitted remain in effect.

### B5. Other terms

Part A, Sections 8, 9, 10 and 11, apply to this Part B, with "You" meaning the Entity.
